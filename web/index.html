Spot check

How good are the labels?

Listen to a few random labelled windows instead of everything. Mark a window wrong if a speaker is missing or labelled when silent for more than about half a second, a turn goes to the wrong speaker, or one person is split into two. Otherwise it is correct. Thirty windows give an accuracy estimate within about ±12 points.

Dataset

Keep the labels as ground truth.

Accepted recordings become an evaluation set: audio, RTTM labels, and a UEM with the regions to score, loadable as a pyannote protocol. Push it to a private Hugging Face repo, then score any checkpoint on it. The scores are per recording, per channel and pooled, with intervals that show whether one checkpoint really beats another. The split sizes below apply only with "Evaluation only" off.

    How it is scored
    
      
    
        
    Metrics
    
          
    
            
    DER
    (missed + false alarm + confusion) ÷ reference speech, after the best one-to-one matching of your speakers to the reference's. Lower is better.
    
            
    Missed
    The reference has more speakers talking than yours.
    
            
    False alarm
    Yours has more speakers talking than the reference.
    
            
    Confusion
    Speech is detected but given to the wrong speaker.
    
            
    JER
    Jaccard error rate: per reference speaker, how badly it is matched, averaged. Every speaker counts the same, however long they talk.
    
          
    
        
    Conditions
    
          
    
            
    Collar
    ±0.25 s around every reference boundary is not scored (the usual forgiving setting). "No collar" is the strict one.
    
            
    Skip overlap
    Leave out the time where the reference has several speakers at once.
    
            
    Reference count
    Your system told how many speakers the reference found. The gap to "Own count" is the cost of guessing the number of speakers.
    
            
    All recordings
    The components are summed over the recordings, then divided, so long recordings weigh more.
    
          
    
        
    Labels
    
          
    
            
    Labels
    DiariZen's turns, used only in the kept regions.
    
            
    Kept
    Nemotron has exactly the same speakers active there (after matching its speakers to DiariZen's), at least 0.25 s from any disagreement, not music without speech, in pieces of at least 1 s.
    
            
    Accepted
    At least half of DiariZen's speech is kept, and there are at most 8 speakers (Nemotron's limit).
    
            
    Vs. labels
    Your system scored only inside the kept regions: the "ground truth" score.
